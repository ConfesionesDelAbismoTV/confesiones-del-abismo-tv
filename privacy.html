Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Confesiones del Abismo TV respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when the channel's publishing application is used.

Information we access

When you authorize the application to connect to a TikTok account, the application may receive authorization information and account-related data made available through TikTok's official APIs. This information is used only to authenticate the authorized account and perform requested content-publishing actions.

Video content

The application may upload video files selected or generated for Confesiones del Abismo TV. It does not sell video content or personal information.

Data sharing

Information is not sold or rented. Data may be transmitted to TikTok only as necessary to provide requested publishing functionality through TikTok's official APIs.

Security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and authorization tokens. Access can be revoked through the relevant platform. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate and troubleshoot the publishing service.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official contact channel associated with Confesiones del Abismo TV.
